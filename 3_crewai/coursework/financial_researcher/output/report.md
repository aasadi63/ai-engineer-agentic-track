## Executive Summary

CCC Intelligent Solutions Holdings Inc. (“CCC” or “CCCIS”) is a scaled, profitable cloud software platform serving the U.S. property & casualty (P&C) insurance ecosystem, with especially strong positioning in auto claims, collision repair, OEM/parts workflows, and increasingly AI-enabled claims automation. Based on the latest available company disclosures through mid-2026, CCC appears to be in **strong operational health** and remains one of the more durable vertical SaaS names in insurance technology.

The company’s recent results show a consistent combination of:
- **Double-digit revenue growth**
- **Adjusted EBITDA margin strength above 40% on a full-year basis**
- **Steady customer and product expansion**
- **Strategic AI positioning through EvolutionIQ**
- **Favorable industry tailwinds tied to claims complexity and workflow automation**

CCC’s business model remains attractive because it is built around mission-critical, recurring workflows with high switching costs and strong ecosystem connectivity. At the same time, the company faces several meaningful risks, including valuation sensitivity, integration complexity from acquisitions, competition in insurance technology, and dependence on the health of the auto/P&C claims environment.

**Overall assessment:** CCC currently looks like a high-quality, growth-oriented vertical software business with strong profitability, good execution, and meaningful long-term optionality from AI and adjacent claims expansion.

## 1) Company Overview

CCC Intelligent Solutions is a cloud-based platform that connects participants across the P&C insurance and auto repair ecosystem. Its software helps insurers, repairers, OEMs, parts suppliers, and related stakeholders streamline claims handling, estimating, workflow coordination, and communications.

### Core business characteristics
CCC’s economic profile is typical of a strong vertical SaaS company:
- Recurring revenue base
- Sticky enterprise relationships
- Mission-critical workflow integration
- Strong scalability
- Attractive operating leverage over time

### Market role
CCC sits at the center of a complex and highly fragmented claims environment. That positioning is strategically valuable because it allows the company to act as a digital workflow layer across multiple participants in the insurance and repair process.
